PERSONAL DESKTOP APPLICATION

Prepare, review, and upload promotional content responsibly.

SocialHub Affiliate helps its owner prepare captions, select local MP4 videos, connect an authorized TikTok account, and submit videos as TikTok drafts for final review.

Private owner-operated integration
01

Explicit authorization

The TikTok account owner signs in and grants access through TikTok Login Kit.

02

User review

Videos, captions, privacy choices, and commercial-content disclosure are reviewed before submission.

03

Local-first storage

Application data and credentials remain on the owner's computer unless an external service is deliberately used.

HOW IT WORKS

A clear owner-controlled workflow

  1. Connect an owned TikTok account using OAuth and PKCE.
  2. Select a local video and review the promotional caption.
  3. Upload the video to TikTok as a draft.
  4. Complete the final review and publication within TikTok.
