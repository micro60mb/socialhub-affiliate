Privacy Policy

This Policy explains how the owner-operated SocialHub Affiliate desktop application handles information.

1. Information processed

When the user connects TikTok, the application may process the TikTok account's basic profile information, including the account identifier, display name, and avatar, together with OAuth access and refresh tokens. It also processes content deliberately selected by the user, such as captions, schedules, local media files, affiliate links, and publishing status.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized account, prepare user-requested content, upload selected media, check publishing status, refresh authorization tokens, display operational history, and diagnose errors.

3. Local storage

SocialHub Affiliate is a local-first desktop application. Its database, media, settings, tokens, and logs are stored on the owner's Windows computer. The website you are viewing does not provide user accounts, collect form submissions, use advertising cookies, or run analytics trackers.

4. Sharing and external services

Information is sent to TikTok only when necessary to complete an action requested by the user. If the owner deliberately enables an optional cloud-storage or artificial-intelligence provider, selected content may also be sent to that provider under its own privacy terms. Personal information is not sold.

5. Token security

OAuth state validation and PKCE are used during TikTok authorization. Access and refresh tokens are intended to be stored encrypted when the application's local secret key is configured. Client secrets and tokens must never be committed to the public website repository.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are retained locally until access is revoked, the account is disconnected, or the local data is deleted. Drafts, media, logs, and publishing history remain locally until removed by the device owner or according to the owner's backup and cleanup practices.

7. User choices and rights

The account owner can decline authorization, revoke TikTok access, disconnect an account, remove local content, and stop using the application. Requests concerning TikTok-held information should also be directed to TikTok through its privacy controls.

8. Children's privacy

The application is not directed to children and is intended only for an adult owner who is eligible to operate the connected accounts and affiliate activities.

9. Policy updates

This Policy may be revised when the application's data handling or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the current published version.

10. Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the public support page. Do not include access tokens, client secrets, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
